# Fixture generation prompt — 30/60/90 plan scenarios

Per `docs/constitution.md` principle VII (fabricated data only, prompt committed beside it).

**Prompt used to fabricate `startup.json` and `turnaround.json`:**

> Write two 30/60/90-day milestone plans for a fictional new leader, one for someone who
> diagnosed their situation as a Watkins "Startup" (building a function from nothing) and one
> for someone who diagnosed a "Turnaround" (reversing a demonstrably failing situation). Each
> plan needs exactly six milestones, two per phase (Days 1-30, Days 31-60, Days 61-90). The
> Days 1-30 milestones for the two plans must be substantively different in focus, not just
> reworded — a Startup's first 30 days is about assessment, hiring, and foundational scoping;
> a Turnaround's first 30 days is about stabilizing the acute failure and rebuilding trust with
> stakeholders who've already lost confidence. Every milestone needs a short rationale that
> explicitly names the situation type it's responding to. No real company, person, or
> statistic — everything fabricated.

Both fixture files below were authored directly against this prompt's brief.
